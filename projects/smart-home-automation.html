AI-Based Smart Home Automation

Exploring intelligent, sensor-informed home automation and more efficient use of available energy.

ESP32C/C++SensorsAutomation

Project overview

This ongoing Computer Engineering project investigates how household appliances can respond to environmental conditions and available energy. It is motivated by energy-management challenges, including scheduling appliances when solar energy is available.

Engineering goal

Explore an ESP32-based system that makes selected household functions more responsive to sensor data and user control.

What I worked on

  • Developed the system concept and automation requirements for household appliances.
  • Worked on circuit planning and simulation for an ESP32-based prototype.
  • Investigated light sensing with an LDR as an indicator of available light, rather than treating it as a direct measurement of solar power.
  • Explored how sensor inputs, appliance schedules and controller decisions could be combined.

Approach and technologies

The project uses embedded-system design, sensor inputs and programmed control logic. Planned AI-related features should be evaluated and documented separately from rules-based automation.

Outcome and learning

Work remains in progress; design, simulation and prototype development can be documented here as they are validated.
